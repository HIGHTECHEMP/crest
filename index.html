Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?
Go to Account → Security → Change Password. You will need to verify your identity via email or SMS, then admin approval.
What are the transfer limits?
Daily transfer limit is €50,000 for verified accounts. You can request a limit increase through support.
How long do deposits take?
Crypto deposits are reviewed by our admin team and typically confirmed within 10-60 minutes. SEPA transfers are instant.
Is my money insured?
Yes, all deposits are insured up to €100,000 under the European Deposit Insurance Scheme.
